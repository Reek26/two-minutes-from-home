A family checklist for the next siren

72-Hour Tornado Plan

From Two Minutes from Home · Temple, Texas · May 22, 2024

Nobody was killed when two tornadoes hit Temple on May 22, 2024. An EF-2 and an EF-1 tore through about 500 homes. This is not a panic sheet. Fill it out once, print it, put it on the fridge.

When the sirens go

  • Phone is charged. Battery pack is in reach.
  • Everyone in the house is accounted for. Say names out loud.
  • Pets located. Crate, carrier, or leash is already in the shelter room.
  • Shoes on. Hard-soled.
  • Move to the shelter spot. Do not wait for a second warning.
  • Stay put until the roar stops and you have a local all-clear.

The rule from that night

Do not try to outrun it in a car. If you are already home, you stay. If you are not, take the nearest solid shelter. A bathroom is better than a canopy. A canopy is better than the open road.

Shelter

  • Interior room, lowest floor, as many walls as you can get. No windows.
  • Helmets, couch cushions, a mattress — cover heads.
  • Keep shoes on. Do not open the door to “see it.”

Power

  • Flashlights that are not a phone. Fresh batteries in the same bin.
  • One battery bank stays charged. Check it on the first of the month.
  • Fridge stays closed. First four hours are fine. After that, eat what thaws.
  • Cash in small bills. Cards die with the power and the towers.

Go-bag · one per adult

  • Copies of IDs and insurance (paper, not only a phone).
  • Meds for three days, plus a list of doses.
  • Phone charger and a short cable. Headlamp.
  • Cash in small bills. N95 or dust masks. Work gloves.
  • Basic first aid. Battery or crank weather radio.
  • One change of clothes. This printed plan, filled in, in a zip bag.

Family comms

Text, do not call, when you can. Pick one person who does not live in your county.
